What Wave 4 adds to the parameter story

  • Memory can be recovered by changing minibatches, but that does not guarantee faster learning. At 6k/75, M5 OOMs while M6 and M10 finish at 63.19% and 62.06% (seed A). At 6k/100, M10 finishes at 66.88% where M5 OOMed. At fixed M6, the 50-step seed-A result is 69.12%, above the 75-step result. Fitting a longer rollout is useful capacity evidence, not a new learning record.
  • Reducing epochs did not rescue 6k/75/M5. E2, E3 and E4 all OOMed. Lower epoch count reduces reuse and compute, but it was not the decisive memory control for this cell. Even 8k/100/M10 failed, after two logged updates. Several new failures occurred after one or two updates, so an initially successful compiled update does not prove sustained memory safety.
  • Three epochs remain the strongest tested general choice, with exceptions and seed noise. At 10k/25, seed-A scores are 55.37%, 61.40% and 60.38% for E2/E3/E4. At 4k/100, E3 averages 64.13% versus 63.28% for E4; E4 wins on seed B and loses on A. This supports the general direction without claiming E3 wins every seed or every regime.
  • Minibatches are an optimization parameter too. At 6k/50, moving M5→M6 lowers samples per minibatch from 60,000 to 50,000 and increases optimizer steps per rollout from 15 to 18. The measured paired mean improves. Further subdivision to M10 is not supported as a better default: its existing single B score is 69.18%, versus 74.01% for M6 on the same seed. At 4k/100, M10 beats M5 on A by only 0.58 points.
  • There is still no measured full boundary around M6. M5 and M10 bracket its minibatch count, but its N/T/E neighborhood is incomplete. In this implementation the environment count must be divisible by the minibatch count: 4k and 8k with M6 fail that constraint, so the old 2k environment grid cannot simply be reused with M6. The visible grid stays at M5 as requested.

Curriculum progression now reinforces the leading pair

The M6 seeds introduce seven-agent targets at 3h40m and 3h18m, both within budget. M5 introduces them at about 4h07m and 6h34m, beyond the cutoff. This supports the M6 choice through a second signal. It is not equivalent to full-task validation: 4k/75 seed A reaches that curriculum stage around 3h46m yet peaks at only 65.36% validation. None of the complete cohorts reaches 75% validation within the strict first four hours.

Decision

Use 6000 / 50 / 3 / 6 for the next training run if optimizing the declared four-hour best-validation objective. M5 remains the more consistent two-seed alternative. The new cells support staying near 6k/50; they do not justify more environments or longer rollouts as a general speed improvement. No broad follow-up wave is prepared. A claim of a uniquely optimal combination would still need stronger seed evidence and additional interacting boundaries.
