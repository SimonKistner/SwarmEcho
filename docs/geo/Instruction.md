# Geo experiment research records

When planning, implementing, running or interpreting probe
experiments (latent/spatial), maintain the research record in `docs/geo/Phase0.md`, `Phase1.md`,
`Phase2.md` and subsequent numbered phase documents as needed. Follow the
record-keeping practice in `docs/geo/Phase0.md`.

Record designs before execution, and actual runs/results/interpretations after
execution. Link original named timestamped artifacts and provenance; do not copy
raw data or source scripts into docs. Separate evidence, hypotheses, proposals
and unresolved decisions. Preserve previous protocols/results when designs change.
Keep Phase 2 an unscoped idea backlog until work is explicitly scoped.

Use the main `docs/README.md` as the index; do not add a separate geo README.
This documentation practice does not authorize additional experiment runs.
