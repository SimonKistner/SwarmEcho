## Deferred cleanup backlog

Keep these options available in this cleanup pass. Revisit their necessity later as separate work.

**Success-weighted target and map sampling.** Keep adaptive cell spawning, adaptive map weighting and their supporting journals, diagnostics and checkpoint restore pending a decision. Both options stay disabled in G03, which uses uniform map sampling and uniform target sampling within the groups allowed by the forward curriculum. Full-budget target eligibility and per-map required-agent histograms are part of the maintained G03 path.

**Randomized base.** Keep this option for checkpoint evaluation and training experiments. G03 leaves it disabled. Geometry clearance applies to sampled base, drone launch and target positions. The generated map’s advanced target exclusions remain tied to its authored base. Decide how to exclude trivial targets next to the sampled base before using randomized-base results for comparison. The current held-out validation and target-eligibility paths require fixed bases. Randomized-base training needs a compatible evaluation setup or changes to those paths.

**Alternate training-map sources.** Keep authored-map training for custom layouts and hand-picked suites, direct training on generated buildings for experiments without a persistent bank, and optional copies of training maps for run provenance. G03 continues to train from compiled banks with automatic selection and generation. Preserve standalone checkpoint evaluation and inspection on custom and off-bank maps.

**Adaptive or curriculum-coupled inspection.** Keep the alternate inspection target distributions. G03 uses independent targets. When simplifying early stopping, preserve inspection sampling independently and check any adaptive curriculum progression that currently shares its stop-source setting.

**Same-map validation and intermediate heatmaps.** Keep full-task same-map validation and empty-group handling for authored maps, plus the optional heatmaps produced during training. G03 uses separate validation maps and final artifacts only.

**Uniform full-budget target sampling.** Keep the forward-curriculum toggle’s off setting for training across all eligible target groups from the start.

**Execution, recovery and diagnostics alternatives.** Defer investigation of alternative OOM strategies, legacy decrement, time limits and profiling, schedule and logging controls, weights-only `init`, and manual checkpoint offsets. Keep execution chunking, stage time limits, checkpoint resume, and historical checkpoint loading available.
